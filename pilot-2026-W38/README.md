# Five Grand Endeavors — 2026-W38 Report

> *Science has a communication problem. It reports isolated discoveries, not progress towards the big outcomes. This newsletter updates humanity on the progress of science towards such outcomes — just as a company would report its progress to its board.*

This edition covers the reporting period 2026-W38 (14–20 September 2026). It covers one endeavor: **Climate and Environment**. Robots and Automation (Software and Hardware), Rockets and Space, Fusion and Energy, Health and Lifespan, and the Supplemental sections (The Knowledge Beyond; Society and Cohesion) are **not covered** this period, as no section reports were produced for them.

## Climate and Environment

*Unlike the other four endeavors, this one is about fixing the past rather than improving the future: humanity messed up the Earth, and we now have to clean up our mess if we are to survive into a better future.*

**KPI: Atmospheric CO₂ concentration and 10-year trend** — **427.55 ppm** (NOAA Mauna Loa monthly mean, August 2026; preliminary), **+2.07 ppm** on August 2025 (425.48 ppm); 10-year trend **2.56 ppm/yr** (Mauna Loa, mean of NOAA Jan→Dec growth rates, 2016–2025). No new monthly or annual NOAA value was published this week. KPI assessment: 🔴 Worsening (unchanged; assessed 2026-07-14; a re-assessment is due).

A Carbon Brief analysis *projects* global fossil CO₂ emissions to fall by about 0.5% in 2026, driven by the Strait of Hormuz crisis. CREA says indicators point to a broad-based decline in China's emissions in August 2026: thermal power -4% (coal power -5.2%), cement -12%, crude steel -4% and refinery throughput -7% year on year. IRENA counts a record 693 GW of renewable capacity added worldwide in 2025 (513 GW solar PV, 158 GW wind), bringing the total to about 5.15 TW. Greensand entered commercial operation on 18 September 2026 as the EU's first full-scale offshore CO₂ storage site, with up to 400,000 tCO₂ per year in its first phase. Google agreed to its largest carbon removal purchase to date: 1 million tonnes of enhanced rock weathering removal from Terradot, to be delivered by 2040.

**However:** The projected 2026 fossil CO₂ fall is shock-driven, and the IEA expects the fall in oil demand to rebound above the 2025 level in 2027. The daily Niño 3.4 anomaly (NOAA ONI convention) reached an all-time record of 3.11°C on 21 September 2026, beating the 3.08°C set on 18 November 2015; the CO₂ KPI assessment's expectation of slower growth in 2026 rests on a Met Office forecast premised on La Niña-like conditions, so that assessment is now under question. India's CO₂ rose 3.7% year on year in H1 2026, driven by an 8% rise in steel and cement emissions. China's thermal power capacity additions in January–July were the highest for that period in 15 years. Technology-based removal credits contracted through long-term offtakes fell to about 6 million in January–August 2026, from 24 million a year earlier. Global energy intensity improved by only 1.7% in 2025, against the 4% per year goal. The Planetary Health Check 2026 finds seven of nine planetary boundaries transgressed, all with worsening trends.
