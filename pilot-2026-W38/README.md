# Five Grand Endeavors — 2026-W38 Report

> *Science has a communication problem. It reports isolated discoveries, not progress towards the big outcomes. This newsletter updates humanity on the progress of science towards such outcomes — just as a company would report its progress to its board.*

This edition covers the reporting period 2026-W38 (2026-09-14 to 2026-09-20; developments published up to 2026-09-22). It covers one endeavor: **Climate and Environment**. Robots and Automation (Software and Hardware), Rockets and Space, Fusion and Energy, Health and Lifespan, and the Supplemental sections (The Knowledge Beyond; Society and Cohesion) are **not covered** this period: no section reports were produced for them.

## Climate and Environment

*This endeavor is unlike the other four: it is about fixing the past rather than improving the future. Humanity messed up the Earth, and we now have to clean up our mess if we are to survive into a better future.*

**KPI: Atmospheric CO₂ concentration (ppm) and 10-year trend (ppm/year)** — Mauna Loa monthly mean CO₂ (NOAA MLO) **427.55 ppm** (2026-08; preliminary); year ago 425.48 ppm (2025-08), change **+2.07 ppm**; change vs previous cutoff +0.00 — ⚠️ not comparable (same observation, no new data). 10-year trend (mean of NOAA MLO Jan→Dec growth rates, 2016–2025) **2.56 ppm/yr**; change vs previous cutoff +0.00 — ⚠️ not comparable (same observation, no new data). **Assessment: 🔴 Worsening** (as of 2026-07-14; unchanged, no new assessment since then).

IRENA reports that a record 693 GW of renewable power capacity was added worldwide in 2025 (513 GW of solar PV and 158 GW of wind), bringing total installed renewable capacity to about 5.15 TW. CREA's indicators point to a broad-based decline in China's emissions in August 2026: thermal power generation −4%, including coal power −5.2% (its second consecutive monthly fall), cement output −12%, crude steel −4% and refinery throughput −7% year on year. Carbon Brief *projects* that global fossil CO₂ emissions will fall by about 0.5% in 2026, driven by the Strait of Hormuz crisis — a projection, not an achieved decline. Greensand entered commercial operation on 18 September 2026 as the EU's first full-scale offshore CO₂ storage site, with capacity of up to 400,000 tCO₂ per year in its first phase. Google made its largest carbon removal purchase to date: 1 million tonnes of enhanced-rock-weathering removal from Terradot, to be delivered by 2040.

**However:** No new KPI headline data arrived this week. India's CO₂ emissions grew 3.7% year on year in H1 2026, with steel and cement emissions up 8%. China's thermal power capacity additions reached 50.3 GW in January–July 2026, the highest for that period in 15 years. Technology-based CO₂ removal credits contracted through long-term offtakes fell to about 6 million in January–August 2026, from 24 million a year earlier (Argus). Global energy intensity improved by only 1.7% in 2025; the goal is 4% per year, so meeting it by 2030 now requires 5.6% per year. ExxonMobil raised its 2050 projection for global energy-related CO₂ to 30 billion tonnes, up from 27 billion tonnes a year earlier. The daily Niño 3.4 sea-surface temperature anomaly hit an all-time record of 3.11°C on 21 September 2026; the KPI rationale relies on a Met Office forecast premised on La Niña-like conditions, and the KPI assessment has not been revisited since. The Planetary Health Check 2026 finds seven of nine planetary boundaries transgressed, all with worsening trends.
