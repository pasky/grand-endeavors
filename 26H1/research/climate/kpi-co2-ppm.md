# KPI: Atmospheric CO2 concentration (ppm) — 26H1

KPI tracking atmospheric CO2 (Mauna Loa / NOAA), the May 2026 seasonal peak, recent
annual growth rate, and the ~10-year trend.

**Status: WORSENING** — CO2 hit a new all-time record in the May 2026 seasonal peak
(432.34 ppm, Mauna Loa); concentration keeps rising every year. The 2025 annual growth
(+2.23 ppm) eased off the 2024 El-Niño-boosted record (+3.33 ppm) but remains squarely
within the long-run ~2.5–2.6 ppm/yr trend. No bending of the concentration curve.

## Headline 26H1 claims

- **May 2026 Mauna Loa monthly mean = 432.34 ppm**, the highest in the 68-year record
  and the annual seasonal peak; vs May 2025 = 430.51 ppm (NOAA "Last updated: Jun 05, 2026").
  https://gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends/
- Independent Scripps instruments measured the May 2026 peak at **432.00 ppm** (+1.8 ppm
  over May 2025's 430.2 ppm); NOAA reported **432.3 ppm** (also +1.8 ppm). Ralph Keeling
  (Scripps CO2 Program): "Atmospheric CO₂ has continued its relentless rise over the past
  year, reaching yet another record high and moving us deeper into a high-CO₂ world. I
  wish we had better news." (Scripps/UCSD, June 11, 2026)
  https://today.ucsd.edu/story/annual-carbon-dioxide-peak-reaches-432-parts-per-million
- Most recent weekly Mauna Loa value (week of June 14, 2026) = 431.17 ppm; the seasonal
  decline from the May peak has begun.
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_weekly_mlo.txt
- Latest complete-year annual mean (Mauna Loa) = **427.35 ppm for 2025**; NOAA global
  marine surface annual mean = **425.65 ppm for 2025**.
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_annmean_mlo.txt
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_annmean_gl.txt

## Growth rate / trend

- 2025 annual growth (Mauna Loa) = **+2.23 ppm**; down from the record back-to-back jumps
  of +3.32 ppm (2023) and +3.33 ppm (2024, El Niño boosted).
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_gr_mlo.txt
- 2025 global growth = **+2.13 ppm**; 2024 was a record +3.74 ppm globally.
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_gr_gl.txt
- 10-year mean growth 2016–2025 ≈ **2.57 ppm/yr (Mauna Loa)** / **2.54 ppm/yr (global)**
  (computed from the NOAA growth-rate files above) — i.e. the rise is still accelerating
  vs the ~2.0 ppm/yr of the 2000s, not slowing.
