## Growth rate / trend

- Annual growth rate 2025 = **+2.23 ppm/yr** (uncertainty ±0.11).
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_gr_mlo.txt

- 2024 growth = +3.33 ppm/yr and 2023 = +3.32 ppm/yr — the two largest annual
  jumps in the 1959–present record (El Niño + high emissions). 2025's +2.23 is
  a step down from those record spikes but remains well above the ~1.6 ppm/yr
  of the 2000s; the long-term growth rate is still accelerating decade over
  decade (no sign of flattening in the concentration curve).
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_gr_mlo.txt

## Status assessment

**WORSENING.** Atmospheric CO2 continues its uninterrupted rise — May 2026 set
a fresh record monthly peak (432.34 ppm) and every recent year sets a new annual
high. The 2025 growth rate (+2.23 ppm/yr) eased from the 2023–24 record spikes
(~3.3) but remains historically elevated; concentration shows no flattening.
