# KPI: Atmospheric CO2 concentration (ppm) + trend (ppm/yr) — 26H1 (Jan–Jun 2026)

Measure note: this is atmospheric CONCENTRATION (dry-air mole fraction, ppm), not emissions.
Two stations/series are used and must not be mixed: **Mauna Loa (MLO)** = single NH
subtropical site (higher, bigger seasonal cycle); **NOAA global mean** = marine surface
sites, lags MLO by ~1–2 ppm. Two "annual growth" definitions also exist: NOAA "growth
rate" = Jan 1→Dec 31 change (Dec/Jan mean difference); "annual-mean increment" = change
between calendar-year averages (used by Met Office/Scripps). Labelled below.
Caveat: NOAA data files were fetched Sep 2026 (file creation 5 Sep 2026); recent months
are preliminary and subject to recalibration. The June 2026 monthly value would only have
been published in early July (just after the period end).

## Trend (ppm/yr)

- **10-yr average growth 2016–2025: MLO 2.56 ppm/yr; global 2.53 ppm/yr** (computed as the
  mean of NOAA annual Jan→Dec growth rates below). The 2020s so far run slightly faster
  (MLO 2021–25 avg 2.61 vs 2016–20 avg 2.51). Met Office cites decadal averages 2000s 1.91,
  2010s 2.41 ppm/yr and 2.61 ppm/yr observed for "2020-2025" / "first 5 years of the 2020s" (Scripps basis).
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_gr_mlo.txt
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_gr_gl.txt
  https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/research/climate/seasonal-to-decadal/seasonal-forecast/forecasts/co2-forecast
- **2025 growth increment (NOAA Jan→Dec): MLO +2.23 ppm, global +2.06 ppm** — a clear
  slowdown after the record years (MLO 2023 +3.32, 2024 +3.33 = record; global 2024 +3.76 =
  record). Global 2025 is the smallest since 2014 (2.03). On an annual-mean basis MLO
  2025 − 2024 = +2.74 ppm (Met Office/Scripps: +2.68), vs +3.53 in 2024.
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_gr_mlo.txt
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_gr_gl.txt
  https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_annmean_mlo.txt
- **No new acceleration record in 26H1; growth decelerating, attributed to La Niña.** Met Office
  forecasts a 2025→2026 MLO annual-mean rise of **2.37 ± 0.55 ppm** ("slightly slowed by a
  temporary strengthening of natural carbon sinks associated with moderate La Niña-like
  conditions"; 2.56 ppm without La Niña), still **"too fast to track IPCC 1.5°C scenarios"**
  (IPCC C1 1.5°C scenarios require a 2020s decadal average of 1.33–1.79 ppm/yr).
  https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/research/climate/seasonal-to-decadal/seasonal-forecast/forecasts/co2-forecast

## Status

**Worsening** — concentration hit new records in 26H1 (432.34 ppm May monthly, 433.95 ppm
daily), though the rate of rise temporarily slowed (~1.5–2.3 ppm YoY, La Niña) from the
2023–24 record pace; the 10-yr trend (~2.5 ppm/yr) is not decelerating structurally.
