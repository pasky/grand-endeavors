kpi-co2-ppm | Research atmospheric CO2 concentration (ppm) as of Jan-Jun 2026 (latest NOAA/Scripps Mauna Loa monthly values and global mean, the May 2026 seasonal peak, 2025 annual average) AND its trend in ppm/year (the 10-year average growth rate plus the latest annual 2025 growth increment vs. prior years), noting any new records or acceleration reported in 26H1.
milestone-the-bend | Research whether the year of peak global greenhouse-gas emissions is definitively behind us as of end of June 2026, using 26H1 evidence such as final/updated 2025 global emissions estimates (Global Carbon Budget, IEA, Carbon Monitor), China's and other major emitters' emissions trends, and expert assessments of whether global emissions have peaked or are projected to fall in 2026.
