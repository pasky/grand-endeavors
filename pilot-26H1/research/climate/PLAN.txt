kpi-co2-ppm | Latest atmospheric CO2 concentration (ppm) through Jan-Jun 2026 (NOAA Mauna Loa and global monthly means, the May 2026 seasonal peak and any record thresholds crossed), plus the growth trend: the 10-year average ppm/year rate and the most recent annual increases (2025 growth rate, early-2026 year-on-year changes) with drivers such as ENSO and carbon-sink behavior.
milestone-the-bend | Whether the year of peak global greenhouse-gas emissions is definitively behind us as of end of June 2026, using the latest 2025 emissions tallies and 26H1 evidence (Global Carbon Budget, IEA, Carbon Brief/CREA analysis of China and India, EDGAR/Climate TRACE, projections for 2026) to judge if a peak has been confirmed or not.
