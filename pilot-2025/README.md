# Five Grand Endeavors — 2025 Year in Review

> *Science has a communication problem. It reports isolated discoveries, not progress towards the big outcomes. This newsletter updates humanity on the progress of science towards such outcomes — just as a company would report its progress to its board.*

This is the pilot edition — a summary 2025 report across all five Grand Endeavors.

---

## Robots and Automation

*Humans have been looking to automate their lives in one way or another for most of their existence. The current frontier is automating intelligence — the software (AI) and the hardware (robots).*

### Software

**KPI: 80-percentile METR task length equivalent** — **32.3 hours** (GPT-5.1-Codex-Max)

AI capabilities doubled every ~7 months on METR benchmarks. Models now reliably complete tasks taking humans a full workweek. Claude Code crossed $1B revenue. 41% of code is AI-generated or AI-assisted. AI solved a 130-year-old math problem (Lyapunov functions) and achieved IMO gold-medal performance. >75 AI-derived drug molecules are in clinical trials.

**However:** METR's RCT found experienced developers are 19% *slower* with AI tools. 95% of enterprise AI pilots fail to reach production. Hallucination rates on reasoning models are *increasing*. Linux kernel maintainers remain hostile to AI-generated code.

| Milestone | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| **The $10M Solopreneur** | 🟡 Approaching — Magnific AI at $10M ARR with 2 people; no verified solo case with <10 hrs/week oversight |
| **The Linux Kernel Patch** | 🔴 Distant — Largest AI patch merged is ~50 lines (200× short of 10k target); major projects banning AI code |
| **The Nature Author** | 🔴 Distant — All major journals prohibit AI authorship; Sakana AI paper accepted then withdrawn |

### Hardware

**KPI: Largest Active Commercial Fleet** — Agility Digit at GXO (100,000+ totes moved)

2025 marked the transition from "humanoid hype" to "humanoid deployment." Figure 02 completed an 11-month production run at BMW (1,250+ hours, 30,000+ vehicles). Waymo crossed 450,000 weekly robotaxi rides. Baidu achieved per-vehicle profitability in Wuhan. VC investment rose 81% YoY to $1.71B.

**However:** Fleet sizes remain tiny (2–10 units per site typical). Tesla abandoned its 5,000 Optimus target. No city has robotaxis outnumbering human taxis. Battery life stuck at 2–5 hours.

| Milestone | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| **Robotaxi Saturation** | 🟡 Approaching — SF closest at ~50% of licensed taxis; Uber/Lyft still dominate |
| **The Wozniak Test** | 🔴 Distant — π₀.5 generalizes to unseen homes but no coffee-making demo; reliability ~20–50% zero-shot |
| **The Fukushima Test** | 🟡 Approaching — Autonomous navigation demonstrated; manipulation still teleoperated |
| **The Blue Collar Shift** | 🟡 Approaching — Figure 02 ran 10-hour shifts for 11 months but with support staff; not fully autonomous |

## Rockets and Space

*The universe promises many worlds humanity could spread to — removing some eggs from the "one basket," a new age of discovery, making all raw materials cheap, and vast opportunities for community independence.*

**KPI: Cost to LEO** — **$1,500/kg** (Falcon Heavy)

We are in a golden age of spaceflight. SpaceX completed 167 orbital launches — more than any entity in history. Humanity delivered ~3,100 tonnes to orbit (new record). Blue Origin's New Glenn achieved the first non-SpaceX propulsive booster landing. Varda completed five in-space manufacturing missions.

**However:** Permanent lunar bases remain years away. Starship HLS delays pushed crewed landing to 2028. Radiation shielding for Mars transit remains unsolved (~1.2 Sv round-trip, double NASA's career limit). Boeing Starliner stranded astronauts for 9 months.

| Milestone | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| **"The Deluge"** (100k tonnes to orbit) | 🔴 Distant — Currently at 3,141 tonnes (3.1%); requires ~1,000 Starship flights/year |
| **"The Homestead"** (permanent crewed base) | 🔴 Distant — No crewed lunar landing since 1972; Artemis III targeting 2027–2028 |
| **"The Export"** (space-manufactured product sold on Earth) | 🟡 Approaching — Varda 5 missions complete; FDA clinical trials expected 2026–2027 |

## Fusion and Energy

*Energy is the key limiting factor of the material world. It does not have to be. The long-term approach is unlikely to be anything but nuclear fusion — and superconductors make everything easier.*

**KPI: Worldwide average electricity cost** — ~$48–90/MWh wholesale (US/EU); Fusion projected >$150/MWh FOAK

2025 was fusion's breakout year. NIF achieved Q=4.13 (8.6 MJ yield). Private investment surpassed $10B cumulative. Helion began construction of the world's first commercial fusion plant. Renewables overtook coal in global electricity generation for the first time (34.3% vs 33.1%). Deep geothermal crossed to commercial scale — Fervo's Cape Station scaling to 500 MW.

**However:** No fusion plant has yet delivered electricity to a grid. ITER's first plasma slipped to 2034, D-T operations to 2039. The industry needs ~$77 billion more to commercialize.

| Milestone | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| **"The First Watt"** (fusion electricity to grid) | 🟡 Approaching — Expected 2028–2032 if private timelines hold; Helion/CFS leading |
| **"Grid Parity"** (fusion <$100/MWh) | 🔴 Distant — FOAK plants projected >$150/MWh; NOAK $50–100/MWh possible 2040s–2050s |

## Health and Lifespan

*Some people long for immortality, others would just prefer not to die yet. The biggest symbol for fixing this is our journey to cure cancer, but the fight against other diseases and perfected early diagnostics are just as important.*

**KPI: Global Healthy Life Expectancy (HALE)** — **61.9 years** (2021) — down 1.6 years from 2019 peak

2025 was a watershed year for early detection. FDA cleared the first blood-based Alzheimer's diagnostic (91.7% sensitivity). GRAIL's PATHFINDER 2 showed 7× cancer detection increase with multi-cancer screening. OpenAI/Retro Biosciences achieved >50× improvement in stem cell reprogramming efficiency. mRNA cancer vaccines entered Phase 3 trials (44% reduction in melanoma recurrence).

**However:** 10-year survival for metastatic solid tumors remains 8–35%. Dementia prevalence after age 80 is ~17–24% (target: 5%). Demonstrated biological age reversal clusters at 2–4 years (target: 10). Global HALE fell 1.6 years during the pandemic.

| Milestone | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| **Taming Cancer** (80% 10-year solid tumor survival) | 🔴 Distant — Metastatic cases at 8–35% 5-year survival; MSK MMRd immunotherapy-only trial shows ~80% response |
| **Precise Screening** (>50% NCDs caught pre-symptomatic) | 🟡 Approaching — Only ~14% of cancers detected through screening; MCED approaching FDA PMA H1 2026 |
| **Unbroken Mind** (dementia <5% after 80) | 🔴 Distant — Current prevalence 17–24% (4–5× target); anti-amyloid therapies slow but don't stop decline |
| **The Long Middle Age** (≥10 years biological age reversal) | 🟡 Approaching — Best results 2–4 years; OpenAI/Retro >50× reprogramming approach promising |

## Climate and Environment

*This endeavor is unlike the other four: it is about fixing the past rather than improving the future. Many historic civilizations fell to climate change, and we now have to clean up our mess if we are to survive into a better future.*

**KPI: Atmospheric CO₂** — **426.5 ppm** (Nov 2025); trend **+3.5 ppm/year** (2023–2024, largest on record)

The clean energy transition is accelerating faster than predicted. Renewable deployment hit 666 GW in 2024. China's emissions have been flat or falling for 18 months. One in four cars sold globally is now electric. Renewables produced more power than coal for the first time (H1 2025).

**However:** Global fossil CO₂ emissions hit a new record (38.1 GtCO₂ in 2025). Atmospheric CO₂ breached 430 ppm for the first time. 2024 was the first calendar year to exceed 1.5°C. Carbon removal remains at ~0.4% of needed capacity. US withdrew from Paris Agreement; major banks exited Net-Zero Banking Alliance.

| Milestone | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| **"The Bend"** (peak global emissions) | 🟡 Approaching — China emissions flat 18 months; global still rising (+1.1% in 2025) |
| **"The Balance"** (net zero) | 🔴 Distant — 53 GtCO₂e/year to eliminate; 95% of enterprise commitments lack implementation |
| **"The Ceiling"** (below +2°C) | 🟡 Under pressure — 2024 first year to exceed 1.5°C; WMO gives 70% odds 5-year average will too |
