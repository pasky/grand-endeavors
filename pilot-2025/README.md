# Grand Endeavors — 2025 Year in Review

> *Humanity's greatest challenges are also its greatest opportunities. This newsletter tracks progress on the endeavors that will define our future — from curing cancer to reaching the stars, from limitless clean energy to artificial minds that work alongside us.*

## Welcome to the Pilot Edition

This is the inaugural year-end round-up of **Grand Endeavors** — tracking humanity's progress on the transformative challenges of our time. We follow six interconnected domains:

- **Health and longevity** — fighting cancer, dementia, and aging itself
- **Climate and environment** — reversing the damage, reaching net zero
- **Energy** — fusion, deep geothermal, and the path to abundant clean power
- **Space** — from orbit to settlement on other worlds
- **Robotics** — hardware that works alongside humans
- **AI and automation** — software that augments human intelligence

Each domain has its own detailed report with KPIs, milestone tracking, and analysis of what moved (and what didn't) in 2025.

## Executive Summary: The State of Grand Endeavors

### 🔴 Cancer and Healthspan

**The tools are emerging, but the milestones remain distant.** 2025 was a watershed for early detection — FDA cleared the first blood-based Alzheimer's diagnostic, GRAIL's multi-cancer screening showed 7× detection increase, and OpenAI/Retro Biosciences achieved >50× improvement in cell reprogramming efficiency. But metastatic cancer survival remains at 8–35%, dementia prevalence after 80 is 17–24% (target: 5%), and demonstrated biological age reversal clusters at 2–4 years (target: 10).

📄 **[Full Report: Cancer and Healthspan](cancer.md)**

---

### 🟡 Climate and Environment

**The Bend is in sight. We have not yet crossed it.** Renewable deployment hit 666 GW, China's emissions have been flat or falling for 18 months, and 1 in 4 cars sold globally is electric. But fossil CO₂ emissions hit a new record (38.1 GtCO₂), atmospheric CO₂ breached 430 ppm, and 2024 was the first calendar year to exceed 1.5°C. Carbon removal remains at ~0.4% of needed capacity.

📄 **[Full Report: Climate and Environment](climate.md)**

---

### 🟡 Fusion and Energy

**"The First Watt" is approaching — expected 2028–2032.** NIF achieved Q=4.13, private fusion investment surpassed $10B cumulative, and Helion began construction of the world's first commercial fusion plant. Deep geothermal crossed to commercial scale with Fervo's Cape Station. But no fusion plant has yet delivered electricity to a grid, ITER slipped to 2034, and fusion LCOE estimates start above $150/MWh for first plants.

📄 **[Full Report: Fusion and Energy](fusion.md)**

---

### 🟡 Rockets and Space

**Launch is solved. Settlement is not.** SpaceX completed 167 orbital launches (all-time record), humanity delivered ~3,100 tonnes to orbit, and launch costs continue falling ($1,500/kg on Falcon Heavy). Blue Origin's New Glenn achieved first non-SpaceX propulsive landing. But permanent lunar bases remain years away, Starship HLS delays pushed crewed landing to 2028, and radiation shielding for Mars transit remains unsolved.

📄 **[Full Report: Rockets and Space](rockets.md)**

---

### 🟡 Robots (Hardware)

**The commercial humanoid era has begun — Year 1 of deployment.** Figure 02 completed an 11-month production run at BMW (1,250+ hours, 30,000+ vehicles). Agility Digit moved 100,000+ totes at GXO warehouses. Waymo crossed 450,000 weekly robotaxi rides. But fleet sizes remain tiny, the Wozniak Test (robot makes coffee in unseen home) remains unachieved, and battery life is stuck at 2–5 hours typical.

📄 **[Full Report: Robots (Hardware)](robots-hardware.md)**

---

### 🟡 Robots (Software/AI)

**AI capability is accelerating exponentially. Reliability and integration lag behind.** METR task benchmarks show AI can reliably complete tasks taking humans ~30 minutes (80% success) or ~5 hours (50% success). Claude Code crossed $1B revenue. ~25% of Google's new code is AI-assisted. AI solved a 130-year-old math problem and achieved IMO gold-medal performance. But METR's RCT found experienced developers are 19% slower with AI tools, 95% of enterprise AI pilots fail to reach production, and Linux kernel maintainers remain hostile to AI-generated code.

📄 **[Full Report: Robots (Software/AI)](robots-software.md)**
